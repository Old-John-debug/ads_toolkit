Sweep1 | ads_simulation | ParamSweep | (<x>, <y>) | 0 | SweepVar=pwr; Start=<起始功率>; Stop=<终止功率>; Step=<步长>; SimInstanceName=HB1
VAR1 | ads_datacmps | VAR | (<x>, <y>) | 0 |

[网络]
# 固定格式：网络名 = 器件.引脚, 器件.引脚, ...
